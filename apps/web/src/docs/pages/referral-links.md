Your wallet address is your referral ID. There is nothing to register.

## What works today

- **Find a campaign** in the directory and open it. The **Promote this campaign** panel shows what you earn per sale (your wallet fills in automatically when connected).
- **People and agents both use claims.** A referral is a signed claim that names your wallet. Buyers (or the agent buying for them) request it from the claim API; see [Agents and claims](/docs/agents-and-claims).
- **Earnings** are on the Earnings page: what has been paid to your wallet, sales still held, claims issued versus sales that reached a payment, distinct buyers and refunded sales. It reads public on-chain data, so you can look up any address.
- **Nothing to withdraw.** Your commission is paid to your wallet in the same transaction that settles each sale. Noux never holds your earnings.

## What is a preview

The `noux.so/r/<wallet>/<campaign>` links shown in the app are previews. The page that turns such a link into a purchase is not built yet, so a link alone does not take a human buyer through checkout. Today the working path is the claim API.

## Say you are paid

Distributors earn a commission when a buyer they referred pays for the service. If you recommend something and you are paid if someone buys, say so to your audience.
